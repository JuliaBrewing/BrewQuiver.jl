BrewQuiver.jl

The brewing process as a quiver.

BrewQuiver is the engine of the BrewQuiver brewing software, in pure Julia. It describes the brewing process as a quiver: a directed acyclic multigraph whose nodes are the contents of vessels at the stages of the brewing (the lots of malt and hops, the water, the mash, the wort in the kettle, the beer in the fermentor, ...) and whose arrows are the processes between them: transfers, drains, mash rests, boils, fermentations, conditioning.

Unlike a linear recipe, a quiver can split and merge streams, as in parti-gyle brewing, double mashes or blending. And since the state of every node holds conserved quantities only, mixing and splitting are exact, and every gram of water, extract and hop is accounted for over the whole brew.

  pale ─┐                    goldings ─┐
crystal ─┼─▶ mash ─MashRest─▶ rested ─Drain─▶ kettle ─Boil─▶ wort ─Chill─▶ chilled ─Drain─▶ fermentor ─Ferment─▶ beer
 water ─┘                       │               ▲                                         ▲
                                └─▶ grain_bed ─Drain┘                                    yeast
                    sparge water ──▶┘

Features

  • Exact mass balance. Water, extract, alcohol, iso-alpha acids, CO₂, color units and dissolved minerals are conserved: what no arrow takes is kept as a leftover (spent grain, trub, yeast cake), and what the arrows release (evaporated water, CO₂) is counted.
  • Derived quantities with BrewingFormulas.jl: volume, gravity, original gravity, ABV, IBU, color, carbonation, mash pH, with models chosen per brew (Tinseth or Rager, Morey, ...).
  • Units everywhere with Unitful.jl and UnitfulBrewing.jl: 4.5u"kg", 1.052u"sg", 12u"°P", 35u"IBU", 2.4u"vols".
  • Brewing techniques: batch and fly sparging, step mashes, decoctions, hop stands, dry hops, bottle priming and forced carbonation, parti-gyles and blends.
  • Equipment: capacities, dead spaces, grain absorption, evaporation and drain rates.
  • Water chemistry: mineral profiles, salts and acids, residual alkalinity, mash pH.
  • Timeline of the brew day and the fermentation, per vessel.
  • Targets: solve the amounts and settings that give a gravity, a bitterness, a volume.
  • Provenance: the share of every lot in every product.
  • BeerJSON: import recipes as quivers, export quivers as recipes, ingredient libraries with inventory, with BeerJSON.jl.
  • Files of quivers in the JSON Graph Format, with the ingredients as BeerJSON records.

Installation

BrewQuiver is under development and not yet registered. It needs Julia 1.11 or later, and takes its sibling packages from GitHub through the [sources] of its Project.toml:

using Pkg
Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/JuliaBrewing/BrewQuiver.jl")

For the examples you will also want the units and the formulas:

Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/JuliaBrewing/UnitfulBrewing.jl")
Pkg.add(url = "https://github.com/JuliaBrewing/BrewingFormulas.jl")
Pkg.add("Unitful")

A first quiver

using Unitful, UnitfulBrewing, BrewingFormulas, BrewQuiver

pale    = Fermentable("Pale ale malt"; yield = 80u"percent", color = 3u"°L")
cascade = Hop("Cascade"; alpha = 6u"percent")
us05    = Culture("US-05"; attenuation = 78u"percent")

q = Quiver()
add!(q, :pale, pale => 4.5u"kg")
add!(q, :cascade, cascade => 30u"g")
add!(q, :yeast, us05 => 1)
add!(q, :water, water(26u"L"; temperature = 72u"°C"))
add!(q, :mash, :wort; vessel = :mashtun)
add!(q, :kettle, :hopped; vessel = :kettle)
add!(q, :fermentor, :beer; vessel = :fermentor)

connect!(q, [:water, :pale] => :mash)
connect!(q, :mash => :wort, MashRest(temperature = 66u"°C"))
connect!(q, :wort => :kettle, Drain(absorption = 1u"L/kg"))
connect!(q, :cascade => :kettle, Transfer(; remaining = 60u"minute"))
connect!(q, :kettle => :hopped, Boil(time = 60u"minute", evaporation = 4u"L/hr"))
connect!(q, :hopped => :fermentor, Drain(retain = 1u"L"))
connect!(q, :yeast => :fermentor)
connect!(q, :fermentor => :beer, Ferment(temperature = 19u"°C", time = 14u"d"))

res = brew(q)
