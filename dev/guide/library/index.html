Ingredient library

A Library holds ingredients by name, with what is in stock. It is loaded from the fermentable, hop and culture records of BeerJSON files (with their inventory), or built with push!:

lib = Library()
push!(lib, pale; stock = 10u"kg")
push!(lib, crystal; stock = 0.2u"kg")
push!(lib, goldings; stock = 100u"g")
push!(lib, ale; stock = 2)
push!(lib, dextrose; stock = 1u"kg")
Library with 5 ingredients
  Fermentable("Corn sugar"), in stock 1.0 kg
  Fermentable("Crystal 60"), in stock 200.0 g
  Hop("East Kent Goldings"), in stock 100.0 g
  Culture("English ale"), in stock 2 ×
  Fermentable("Pale ale malt"), in stock 10.0 kg

Ingredients are found by name, or by part of it:

(lib["Pale ale malt"], lookup(lib, "crystal"))
(Fermentable("Pale ale malt"), ["Crystal 60"])

Inventory

res = brew(q)
requirements(res, q)          # what the brew uses of each ingredient
Dict{String, Any} with 5 entries:
  "East Kent Goldings" => 0.05 kg
  "Pale ale malt"      => 4.5 kg
  "English ale"        => 1.0
  "Crystal 60"         => 0.3 kg
  "Corn sugar"         => 0.11 kg
shortages(lib, res, q)        # what the stock lacks for it
1-element Vector{@NamedTuple{name::String, needed, available}}:
 @NamedTuple{name::String, needed, available}(("Crystal 60", 0.3 kg, 0.2 kg))

consume! takes what the brew uses from the stock (warning about what is short), and stock! sets it:

stock!(lib, "Crystal 60", 1u"kg")
consume!(lib, res, q)
stock(lib, "Pale ale malt")
5.5 kg

Files

lib = Library("my_ingredients.json", "more.json")   # BeerJSON documents
lib = readjson(Library, "my_ingredients.json")
merge!(lib, other)
writejson("my_ingredients.json", lib)

Quiver(recipe; library = lib) completes the additions of a recipe with the records of the library of the same name: the moisture of fermentables, and the attenuation of cultures.
