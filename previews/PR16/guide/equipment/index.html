Equipment

A Vessel describes the equipment of a vessel:

FieldWhat it is
capacitythe volume it holds, checked by overfilled
dead_spacethe liquid left behind when drained (under a false bottom, the trub in a kettle, the yeast cake)
absorptionthe liquid absorbed by the grain, per mass of grain (a mash tun)
hop_absorptionthe liquid absorbed by hops, per mass of hops (a kettle, a fermentor with dry hops)
evaporationthe evaporation rate of a boil: a volume per hour, or a fraction per hour
drain_ratethe volume drained per unit of time

equip! attaches it to the stages tagged with that vessel (add!(...; vessel = :kettle)). The arrows leaving them take from it what they do not give themselves:

  • a plain Drain() leaves the grain absorption and the dead space behind, and takes the time of the drain rate;
  • Boil(time = 60u"minute") evaporates at the rate of the kettle.

An arrow that gives its own value overrides the vessel: Drain(retain = 2u"L"), Boil(evaporation = 5u"L/hr").

q = single_batch()
equipment(q, :kettle)
Vessel("Kettle"; 36.0 L, 1.0 L dead space, evaporating 4.0 L/h, draining 1.5 L/min)
res = brew(q)
volume(res[:kettle]) - volume(res[:wort])     # 4 L/hr for an hour, and the cooling shrinkage
3.9987913288816586 L

Capacity

overfilled lists the stages whose contents exceed the capacity of their vessel (with the volume the grain displaces in a mash):

equip!(q, :fermentor, Vessel(name = "Small bucket", capacity = 20u"L"))
foreach(println, overfilled(brew(q), q))
(node = :fermentor, vessel = :fermentor, volume = 22.04202369904633 L, capacity = 20.0 L)
(node = :beer, vessel = :fermentor, volume = 21.937745291907937 L, capacity = 20.0 L)

From BeerJSON

The equipment items of a BeerJSON document (mash tun, kettle, fermentor, ...) become the vessels of the quiver of a recipe; see A BeerJSON recipe.
