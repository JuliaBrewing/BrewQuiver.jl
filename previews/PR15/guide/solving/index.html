Targets and solving

solve! changes parameters of a quiver so that its brew meets targets, by Newton's method on its brews.

Targets

A target is node => f => goal, for any function f of the contents of the node (gravity, original_gravity, volume, ibu, srm, abv, carbonation, ...), with the goal in any of its units: a gravity in °P is converted with the gravity model.

What varies

  • Amount(lots...) scales the amounts of lots together, keeping their proportions (a grain bill), with the transfers of masses and volumes leaving them.
  • Setting(source => destination, field) varies a numeric field of an arrow: the amount of a transfer (a blend fraction), the time of a boil, the stand of a hop stand, the temperature of a mash rest, ...

There are as many parameters as targets.

Example

The grain bill, hops, sparge water and priming sugar for an original gravity, a bitterness, a pre-boil volume and a carbonation:

sol = solve!(q, :fermentor => gravity => 1.052u"sg", :wort => ibu => 35u"IBU",
             :kettle => volume => 28u"L", :bottles => carbonation => 2.4u"vols";
             vary = [Amount(:pale, :crystal), Amount(:goldings), Amount(:sparge_water),
                     Amount(:priming_sugar)])
Solution in 3 iterations
  amount of :pale, :crystal: :crystal Contents(302.0 g Crystal 60), :pale Contents(4.531 kg Pale ale malt)
  amount of :goldings: Contents(87.5 g East Kent Goldings)
  amount of :sparge_water: Contents(14.98 L, 1.0 sg, 80.0 °C)
  amount of :priming_sugar: Contents(151.0 g Corn sugar)
  gravity(:fermentor) = 1.0519999999999998 sg (goal 1.052 sg)
  ibu(:wort) = 35.000000000014815 IBU (goal 35 IBU)
  volume(:kettle) = 27.999999999999996 L (goal 28 L)
  carbonation(:bottles) = 2.399999999999974 vols (goal 2.4 vols)

The quiver is changed in place:

q[:pale].initial
Solids: 4.531 kg Pale ale malt

A goal in other units:

solve!(q, :fermentor => gravity => 13u"°P"; vary = [Amount(:pale, :crystal)])
q[:pale].initial
Solids: 4.583 kg Pale ale malt

The Solution holds the iterations, the parameters and the values reached. If the targets cannot be met (e.g. a gravity that would need a negative amount), solve! throws an error, leaving the quiver as it was.

The blend fractions of a parti-gyle are solved the same way; see A parti-gyle.
